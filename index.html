Syllabus

Discrete Mathematics, made interactive

Every topic of the discrete mathematics syllabus – from sets and logic to graphs and trees – turned into a simulator you can play with. Type your own sets, relations, formulas, groups and graphs and watch Venn diagrams, arrow diagrams, Hasse diagrams, Cayley tables, graph drawings and algorithms respond step by step. Each module also links the idea to a real-world use, such as search engines, databases, secure browsing, scheduling and GPS routing.

How to use it

1. Pick a topic

Use the sidebar: units and sub-topics are listed in syllabus order. Every sub-topic is its own interactive card.

2. Change the inputs

Edit the sets, relations, formulas or graphs – or load a preset – and the diagrams redraw instantly.

3. Follow the working

Step-by-step tables, animations and counter-examples show why a result holds, not just the answer.
